About this data

Source

Geological units come from Macrostrat (carto tile layer), licensed CC BY 4.0. Each unit is credited to its original map in the popup.

Scale

The units come from different source maps depending on zoom. The source map under the centre of your view is shown below and changes as you zoom. Zooming in further only enlarges the shapes. It does not add detail.

Limitations

  • Ages are in millions of years ago (Ma).
  • The rock type field was empty on every unit we clicked, so rock type is shown only as the description text.
  • The age filter shows a unit if its age span overlaps your range. Units with no age are hidden by the filter.
  • Age text can be a regional term, such as Namibian, that is not on the international time scale. When it cannot be matched to a precise interval, the numeric age range can span a whole era, for example the Neoproterozoic, 1000 to 538.8 million years ago. That range is not a measured age.
  • The background map is OpenFreeMap, built from OpenStreetMap data. It is a free public service with no guarantee of continuity.
